DWS Station Explorer. Station locations derived from the DWS RQIS water-monitoring KMZ (357 sites carrying a hydrology gauging code, deduplicated; A2H010 verified). The map is not exhaustive of the full ~3000-station network — swap in the DWS hydrology KML to extend it. Flow/level parsed client-side from the DWS SAFMAXLEVELFLOW monthly-maximum export; nothing is uploaded to a server. Quality codes: $ gauge-plate, A above-rating, E estimated, M missing, Q not-audited, blank = verified. Volumes are user-supplied. Map data (c) OpenStreetMap contributors.
